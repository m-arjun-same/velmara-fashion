Style

Nearly twenty seasons have passed since we first took up needle and cloth — from small ateliers where every seam was finished by hand, to runways lit for a global audience.

Through all of it, one conviction has stayed with us: dressing is not the making of beautiful things; it is work that supports expression.

We take the intent inside a house — its history, its hopes, the life it imagines for its wearer — and shape it into the form that fits it best. Cutting is listening. Draping is translation.

Today, generative AI is changing the very nature of making. We see that change not as a threat but as possibility. People think; AI supports. The hand still decides.

Whatever the era, listening to people's intent, organising it, and shaping it into a form that is seen and felt — that is the role that never changes for us.
